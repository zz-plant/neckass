Filing standards

These standards govern what the Gazette files, and in what manner. They were adopted in the paper's first week and have been revised once, in a way no member of staff has been able to identify.

General

  • Where a matter is uncertain, it is reported and then not confirmed.
  • An adjective is not used where a document will do.
  • The Gazette does not describe conduct as amusing. Readers have not required the assistance.
  • Where a subject offers an explanation, the explanation is printed in full and is not evaluated.
  • A filing may take a second sentence. The second sentence is not required to help.
  • Exclamation marks are reserved for the classifieds, where they have never been used.

On the term

  • The Gazette has not defined neckass and does not intend to. Its correspondents report on one daily and remain in agreement about individual cases.
  • The word is set lowercase as a common noun and capitalized as the subject of this paper.
  • No filing may turn on the neck. A 2036 review is expected to find that it does not refer to anything.

Varieties recognized by the desk

Routine neckassery. A minor domestic failure, reported by sources familiar with the matter. Filed daily. The stated intention is contradicted within the same sentence wherever the facts permit.

Administrative neckassery. An institution takes a formal position on a Neckass and later declines to comment. Also covers reviews, studies, and committees that agree about the case while failing to define the term.

Neckass-adjacent. Behavior which experts classify as neckass-coded but stop short of confirming. Also the department under which an object, system, or weather event is described as neckass, with the appropriate caveat.

Unprecedented neckassery. Conduct exceeding all previously documented cases, according to people who document cases. Filed rarely, and twice in error.

Matters the Gazette will not file

  • Any matter in which the subject was, on review, correct.
  • Conduct the subject has already been told about, unless it has recurred, which it has.
  • Anything the printer has asked us to stop covering.
