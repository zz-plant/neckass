From the editor

Why a paper

This paper will report what he did. It will not say why, because nobody knows, least of all him.

— The editor

In brief

  • Neckass says he read the room. Everyone in the room says he did not.
  • Neckass wrote "no rush" and followed up four minutes later.
  • Neckass said he would circle back. That was in March.
  • Neckass told the group he had booked it. He had looked at it.
  • Neckass said "keep the change." There was no change.
  • Neckass's houseplant has outlived three of his hobbies.
  • Neckass responded "maybe" to his own birthday.

Corrections

  • An earlier edition called Neckass's plan a plan. It is three words on a page. The Gazette regrets the error.
  • The Gazette stated that Neckass had finished. He had stopped.
  • We described Neckass's apology as an apology. It was the sentence "well, sorry you feel that way."
  • An earlier edition quoted Neckass as saying "no worries." There were worries.
