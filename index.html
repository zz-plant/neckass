Today's issue

Neckass has been "five minutes away" since 2019. He is at home.

CORBIN FALLS — He has said "five minutes" to eleven people this year. He has not left the house for any of them.

By the wire desk

From the editor

Why a paper

This paper will report what he did. It will not say why, because nobody knows, least of all him.

— The editor

In brief

  • Neckass says he read the room. Everyone in the room says he did not.
  • Neckass wrote "no rush" and followed up eleven minutes later
  • Neckass said he would circle back. That was in March.
  • Neckass told the group he had booked it. He had looked at it.
  • Neckass said "keep the change." There was no change.
  • Neckass's houseplant has outlived three of his hobbies
  • Neckass responded "maybe" to his own birthday

Notices

  • Town council meets Tuesday at 7 p.m. in the council chamber. The agenda is posted at the town office.
  • The Mill Street bridge is reduced to one lane through the end of the month.
  • Recycling collection moves to Thursday this week because of the holiday.

Corrections

  • A caption in Monday's edition misidentified the street shown. It is Mill Street, not Mill Road.
  • An earlier edition called Neckass's plan a plan. It is three words on a page. The Gazette regrets the error.
  • The Gazette stated that Neckass had finished. He had stopped.
  • We described Neckass's apology as an apology. It was the sentence "well, sorry you feel that way."

Weather

High 68°, low 51°. Winds W 6 mph. Sunset 7:10 p.m.

Sunny, according to Neckass, who has not been outside and is going by the window.
