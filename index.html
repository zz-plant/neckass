    About this publication

    The Digital Gazette reports on the activities of a Neckass. Tap Shuffle to be issued a new item. Each may be copied, shared, or exported as a front page.

    The Gazette operates entirely within the browser. Recent items are kept in local storage. No account is required, and none has proved useful.

    Specific varieties are catalogued under neckassery.
