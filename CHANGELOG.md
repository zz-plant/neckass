## [Unreleased]
### Changed
- A continuity pass over the paper. In thirteen leads the letter or ad beside the lead gave away the fact the next morning's correction reveals (the six screws, the folder called "Later!!", the 2:31 arrival, the raisins, the wrong restaurant, the second membership); each now leaves the correction its news. The paper no longer contradicts itself: he lives at No. 12, not in an apartment; No. 366 ran on a Monday and its editorial no longer ends "It is Friday"; the best man answered the email in four minutes and the groom says so; the doctor who would not discuss a patient no longer writes in about him; the gym he has been to twice is no longer one he has never entered; the phone has been at one percent since breakfast, not three days; the goodbye in the doorway is still going at press time; No. 288 no longer retracts a fact it had not printed; and letters no longer sign themselves "again," which the deal cannot promise. Duplicated jokes are cut to one telling (the 11,000 images, the nine-day leftovers, the smart speaker and the lights, the hallway with a bicycle in it, the minute to really reply), and two leads that were one joke are two: the story retold at the same table is now a fourteen-minute wedding toast about himself, and the restaurant he recommended without visiting is a dentist. The civic notices are out, as the guardrail requires: the council's first and third Tuesday is book club, the holiday trash collection is a holiday he went in to work on, and the poll worker, the pickleball courts' notice and "residents may wish to use the back way" are gone. The weakest items now name an incident (he read the room at a baby shower and proposed a drinking game; the houseplant has outlived the guitar, the sourdough starter and the running). "I am his mother" opens three letters, down from six, and "has" no longer stands in for a finished act. Every edit is in place, so no pool changes length and no back issue re-deals; the issues the entry cites still run what it says they run.
- The copy is set in curly quotes and apostrophes (“ ” ’) wherever a reader sees it: the pools, the landmark issues, the calendar, the pages, the front page's back-issue notice and the still cards. Straight quotes had shown as typewriter marks in the 80px headline and on the card. Fraunces and the card's two static cuts carry the glyphs. A smoke test walks every pool and the text of every page for a straight one.
- The social card is drawn once per issue in each data center. The first fetch renders it and stores it in the Workers cache under the issue it resolved to and the deploy (`version_metadata` in `wrangler.jsonc`), so an edit to the copy is drawn at once; every unfurler after that is answered from the cache. In production each fetch had redrawn the card, at about a second each.
- `docs/assets/`, fifteen PNGs and their `source.svg` under the paper's old name ("Neckass DIGITAL GAZETTE", a BREAKING kicker, "Local man opens 19 tabs", "SATIRE HEADLINE GENERATOR"), is removed with its generator (`scripts/generate-scenes.ts`, `scripts/asset-specs.ts`, `bun run assets:generate`); nothing on the site used them. `bun run assets:check` now checks the four social cards and the three icons the pages and the manifest name.
- The file cut is redrawn as a man in a suit, and acts each pose out instead of rocking one joint back and forth. He wears a jacket open over a shirt and a striped tie, with notched lapels, two buttons, flap pockets and a pen in the breast pocket, and creased trousers with a fly; the face is a size larger. The arms hang in front of the jacket, with the tops of the sleeves under its shoulders, so his hands rest on his hips and scratch his head where they used to go behind him, and an arm turns at the shoulder without showing where it ends. Three joints are added, each set by every pose: `lean`, which tips everything above the belt on legs that stay put, and a wrist on each hand. The motion draws back before it goes, makes each beat smaller than the last, moves the wrist after the forearm and the tie after everything, and settles past the pose before it stops. He blinks, and his brows go up when he makes a point; resting his eyes, he nods off with them open, catches himself and blinks twice; standing by, he blinks once. The alarm clock rings in two bursts, the smoke alarm's chirp goes out and the next comes in behind it, and the shelf gives at its bracket. It still plays once, from 0.4 to 4.4 seconds, and not under reduced motion. The figure in all three copies of the drawing is written by `bun run cut:draw` from `scripts/draw-cut.ts`: one set of geometry and a hand that bows each line from a fixed seed. The still cards are redrawn.
- The file cut is redrawn by hand, in what looks like a fine-point Sharpie: one black, nearly one weight of line, round ends, no grey hatching, and no line quite straight, the props included. He has a jaw, an ear, eyebrows, a smirk, a cowlick, lapels, mitten hands with thumbs and rounder shoes. The joints are capsules round on their pivots, so a turned elbow or shoulder no longer shows the corner of a rectangle, and the round tops of the arms are his shoulders. No line is drawn where a pen would not draw one: each arm, and the legs with the hips, is one outline, so there is no seam at the elbow and no line across the trousers under the belt, and in the props a piece that meets another is tucked under it instead of drawing a second edge along it. The head is drawn behind the jacket, so the neck shows in the open collar, with his Adam's apple on it. The still cards are redrawn.
- A pass over the whole paper for the jokes everyone has already heard. The stock observations now go one step further: he says "you too" to the pilot and asks where he is staying; thanks the ATM and, offered another transaction, says "no, you've done enough"; documents his digital detox in fourteen posts and a story asking whether anyone noticed he was gone. Briefs that named a habit and stopped now turn (the meal prep is saved by the takeout, the inbox reaches zero by a rule that marks it read, the "sometime" coffee was accepted in August and not answered). Two briefs that restated a lead ("maybe" to his own birthday, the hat after the barber) are new items. The front page's deck no longer repeats its headline: he has said "five minutes away" to eleven people, including his roommate, who was in the room; the standing issue and `og-image.png` carry it. In the leads, the best-man letter no longer contradicts its own deck, the door correction retracts something, and the restaurant hostess offers him a table four times at what turns out to be the wrong restaurant. Everything is rewritten in place, so no pool changes length and no back issue re-deals.
- The sourced lines in the entry turn, too. Speakers, who were not consulted, describe the neck as a demotion from the head, and no promotions are recorded; the 2036 review did not consult Green's, and its authors, told of it, explained that they had meant a different neck; the 2017 entry's differences from *headass* have not been named since; the December 2017 example is quoted ("I don't [...] know"); and the early instructions to get out, shut up, or stop dancing record no compliance. In the briefs, the victory dance was for the next game, which he lost 21–4, and he saw the lamppost coming.
- Issue links carry an Article schema for the printed lead, marked as satire, with the issue's permalink and social card. Browser and edge metadata share the same source. Back issues keep their dated canonical URL, and a future issue link describes today's paper instead of presenting an unpublished lead to crawlers. No real-world author or publication timestamp is inferred from the paper's fictional byline or folio.
- The site runs as a Cloudflare Worker with static assets instead of a Pages project. `worker.js` routes `/card.png` and `/feed.xml` to the same handlers in `functions/`, matched as Pages matched them, and runs the middleware in front of them and the front page; `wrangler.jsonc` keeps the Pages project's compatibility date, 2024-09-29. The repository root is still the assets directory, and `.assetsignore` is an allowlist of the site's own files: Pages had published everything but `functions/`, so `package.json`, the docs, the tests, the scripts and the workflow were readable at neckass.com, and they now return 404. `_headers` sends the three headers Pages added by default and `_redirects` its 308s, which Workers send as 307. The Worker drops the asset's ETag from the rewritten front page, which would otherwise answer a revalidation with another day's paper, and sends the 404 page as `no-store`, as Pages did. `bun run edge` is `wrangler dev`, `bun run deploy` is `wrangler deploy`, and CI bundles the Worker. Wrangler is 4.150, the first release whose dev server does not reload in a loop when the assets directory is the project root.

The paper now speaks in two voices: every sentence is set in Fraunces, from the lead down to an ad, where the corrections, weather, classifieds, colophon and standards were in the system sans; and the wire's apparatus comes off the teletype in the system monospace, upper case: the folio line, the department heads, bylines, issue folios, the forecast's figures, each ad's keyword, letter signatures, the cutline's FILE and the colophon. The file cut moves from the foot of the briefs to the lead's narrow column, over the earlier issues, where a paper runs the photograph, which also closes the gaps it left beside it and under the weather. The ads run in two narrow columns from 1000px, a month's head in the back issues is set large in Fraunces, and the stock is a greyer newsprint. The standards set the one word they had in italics in quotes, as a wire would. The page adds no font file. The repo's front-page, back-issues and reference screenshots are retaken.

- The paper runs more than one spot colour, and each has one job. The ledger teal is the record: besides the cutoff rule and the links it now sets the issue folios in *Earlier issues* and the back issues, and a screen of it carries the back-issue notice under the cutoff rule. A rubricator's red marks the corrections and the red-letter issues: the Corrections head and the dash before each correction, the editorial's head on a landmark issue, and *Of note* and the landmark tags in the back issues. Cloudflare Pages answers each `.html` address with a 308 to the extensionless one, so the sitemap and the canonical tags pointed search engines at redirects, and every masthead link cost a round trip. `scripts/serve.ts` serves `/archive` from `archive.html` as Pages does, and a smoke test holds the sitemap and the canonicals to the extensionless paths.
- The paper reads less clipped. A rule-based scan flagged 212 strings with stacked short beats: two or more sentences of five words or fewer before the last one, or three short sentences in a row. Fifty-six are rejoined so that each keeps one turn, which the last sentence carries: the lead's deck, correction and follow-up lines, the letters that ran in bursts of "I am." and "He said.", the weather lines, three briefs, and one paragraph of the entry, where the three beats of "It does not happen" are now one sentence with a colon. Briefs, corrections, classifieds, letters, and the forecaster's note are dealt in rounds of their pools, as the leads are, instead of shuffled afresh each morning, when nearly half of a day's classifieds had run in the week before and a brief could run two days in a row.
